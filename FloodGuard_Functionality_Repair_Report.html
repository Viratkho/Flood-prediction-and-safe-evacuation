Phase 1 & 2: Population Analysis Tool Complete Repair

1. Draggable Geographical Circle & Center Marker

Previously, the population tool was stationary and lacked direct circle manipulation. The tool now responds instantly to click events across the Leaflet map and features full bidirectional draggability:

  • Direct Circle Surface Dragging: Administrators can grab the circle anywhere on its border or interior. Leaflet's map pan is temporarily locked (map.dragging.disable()) while delta latitude and longitude are calculated smoothly at 60Hz.
  • Center Pin Marker: Features a pulsing cyan beacon (custom-pop-center-marker) that can also be dragged independently across the basin.
  • Continuous Coordinate Tracking: As either marker or circle is dragged, the center coordinates update continuously in React state (centerRef.current), recalculating bearing and range for all database contacts in real-time.
  • Automatic Metrics Refresh: Upon releasing the drag (mouseup / touchend), the backend endpoint /api/admin/population/analysis is invoked automatically to fetch updated demographic data.

2. Exact 1,000m (1.0 km) Geodesic Initial Radius

The default search radius has been calibrated to exactly 1,000 metres (1.0 km). The UI displays 1,000 m (1.0 km), and clicking the Reset control restores the circle to 1,000m centered at the Avadi Basin hub (13.1143° N, 80.1015° E).

3. Continuous Radius Expansion (Strict 5-Second Interval Rule)

A custom high-precision press-and-hold engine was implemented to enforce the 5-second interval rule with zero fractional drifting:

  • Hold Progression: Initial: 1,000 m → 5s: 1,100 m → 10s: 1,200 m → 15s: 1,300 m → 20s: 1,400 m (+100m for each completed 5 seconds).
  • Strict Interval Enforcement: Radius increases only when a complete 5,000ms threshold is crossed. Releasing the hold at 3.4 seconds immediately discards the partial unearned time and stops expansion at the current earned radius.
  • Touch & Mouse Support: Bound to onMouseDown, onMouseUp, onMouseLeave, onTouchStart, onTouchEnd, and onTouchCancel with touch-action: none to prevent browser scrolling.
  • Visual Live Feedback: Displays active progress bar, timer readout (HOLDING... 3.2s / 5.0s), and an earned step indicator (e.g. +200m earned).

Phase 2 (Cont.): Surveillance Radar Canvas Integration

Surveillance Radar Canvas Overlay (Incorporating radar.html)

The surveillance radar animation from the attached radar.html was adapted into an interactive Leaflet overlay attached to the map's overlayPane (z-index 350, pointer-events: none):

  • 10 Concentric Range Rings: Alternating dashed and solid cyan rings drawn at R * i / 10.
  • Rotating Sweep Line & Conic Glow Trail: A 6.0-second rotating beam with linear gradient and conic gradient trail wedge (createConicGradient) casting tactical bloom over the map.
  • Radial Diagonals & Crosshairs: Compass quadrant crosshairs and 45° radial diagonals.
  • Outer Compass Bezel: High-density bezel with 2° degree ticks, major ticks every 10°, and cardinal bearing markers (N, E, S, W).
  • Real Registered Database Contacts: Rather than simulated blips, contacts are mapped directly from analysis.individuals using true geographic bearing (atan2(dLng, dLat)) and normalized range (distanceM / radiusM).
  • Tactical Blip Illuminations & Reticles: When the sweep beam passes over a contact:
    • Contacts illuminate with bloom and corner bracket reticles.
    • Active SOS alerts display in pulsing high-alert red (255, 59, 48) with expanding detection pulse rings.
    • Residents with declared disabilities display in purple (192, 132, 252).
    • Residents in flooded zones display in amber (251, 191, 36).
    • Hover/tactical tags render name, ID, bearing angle, and distance in km.

Phase 4 & 5: Family Safety & Mutual Map Visualization

1. Request Lifecycle & Payload Normalization

A contract mismatch between camelCase (targetPersonId) and snake_case (connection_id) in backend/api/family.py and FamilySafetyDrawer.tsx was resolved. Both endpoints now accept aliased keys and return fully normalized models. Users can send invitations by FloodGuard ID (e.g. P-102), view pending outgoing/incoming invitations, and accept or reject with immediate state updates.

2. Unified Single-Map Architecture (MyLocationMap.tsx)

Connected family members are rendered directly on the same existing Leaflet map:

  • Distinguishable Avatars: Distinct emerald badges with initial and relationship tags (e.g. "Spouse", "Child").
  • Clustering & Spiderfying: When family members share the same home coordinates, an angular offset algorithm (radius 20m) automatically spiderfies the markers, preventing GPS pin overlap.
  • Status Beacons: High-risk flood exposure displays an amber halo with water depth, and active SOS distress triggers an animated red ping.
  • Focus Action: Clicking Focus in the Family Safety drawer smoothly pans the map (map.flyTo) to that family member's location.
